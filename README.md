# What it costs 💸

A minimal, single-file dividend opportunity cost calculator that runs entirely in the browser — no build step, no dependencies.

## What it does

Enter a **purchase amount** (€) and a **dividend yield** (%), and the calculator instantly shows you what you are giving up by spending that money instead of investing it:

| Result | Formula |
|---|---|
| Lost dividend (1 year) | purchase × yield |
| Lost dividend (20 years) | purchase × yield × 20 |
| Lost wealth equivalent | purchase ÷ yield |

The numbers update in real time as you type. The purchase amount field auto-formats with German thousand separators (e.g. `10.000`).

## Usage

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser. No server required.

## Purpose

The calculator is designed as a psychological nudge: by making the hidden cost of a purchase visible and prominent, it encourages more deliberate spending decisions.
